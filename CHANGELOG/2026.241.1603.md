## The load-bearing decision: what "ready" means

Not *"the process started"* — the two things a browser client depends on: a **DRM render node exists** (no software fallback can host a nested compositor) and the **compositor published a Wayland socket**. `CSTREAM-READY` is emitted only when both hold; `/healthz` returns `503` otherwise.
